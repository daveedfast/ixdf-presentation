Intro

Hello there! I’m a Brazilian-American user experience strategist, interaction designer, and educator—with over 18 years designing and consulting for a variety of startups, agencies, and global brands.

I love looking for unique ways to unlock value for the business by starting with customers’ unmet needs and simplifying interactions to motivate action.

I’m a proud dad to a daughter and 2 pups. I currently live in the Florida Keys where I am an aspiring kiteboarder; learning how to stay on top of the water rather than in it.
